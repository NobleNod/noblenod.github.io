Why this matters

I've taken 100+ technical interviews at Amazon. I've screened dozens of students for my referral pipeline. Most don't make it past my call.

Here's what gets them rejected:

  • Couldn't explain code they wrote

    If you can't explain why you made a decision, you didn't really make it.

  • Couldn't trace their logic without running it

    Guessing and checking isn't debugging. Interviewers watch how you think.

  • Recognized patterns but couldn't explain why they work

    Memorizing solutions isn't understanding. One follow-up question exposes the gap.

  • Resume promised more than they could defend

    Your resume is a promise. The interview is where you prove it.
