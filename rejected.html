Why this happens

I reject candidates for a few reasons:

AI-generated responses. If your submission read like ChatGPT wrote it, you got one chance to rewrite it authentically. If you didn't take that chance, or if the rewrite still felt AI-assisted, I moved on.

Technical fundamentals. If we had a screening call and you couldn't trace through your own code, explain your project's architecture, or work through basic problems, that's a gap I can't bridge with a referral.

Eligibility mismatch. Maybe you're still in college, already employed, or not able to relocate to Chennai. These aren't character flaws. They're just not what I'm filling right now.

No response. If you didn't reply to my follow-up within 48 hours, I closed your application. I move quickly with candidates who engage.

What you can do now

If it was AI slop: Learn to write in your own voice. Your authentic, messy explanation of what you built is more compelling than any polished ChatGPT output. Practice describing your projects like you're talking to a friend.

If it was technical gaps: Go deeper on fundamentals. Don't just memorize patterns - understand why they work. Practice tracing through code by hand. Write recurrence relations. Analyze complexity. Build the muscle.

If it was eligibility: Keep my info. When your situation changes - you graduate, you're open to Chennai, you're looking for your first role - reach out again. I keep all data on file.
